# Component System

## 1. ORCA Glass Dock

Used for:
- left sidebar;
- compact floating controls.

Never use it as a full dashboard sidebar.

## 2. Ask ORCA Bar

Primary interaction component.

States:
- idle;
- focus;
- listening;
- thinking;
- result-ready.

## 3. PFZ Marker

Structure:
- central point;
- one circular ring;
- compact label;
- distance.

Avoid multi-ring glowing targets.

## 4. Boundary Overlay

Types:
- restricted;
- protected;
- hazard;
- international/reference.

Use fine linework and subtle fills.

## 5. Route Line

States:
- recommended;
- alternative;
- rejected.

Recommended:
- clear;
- slightly brighter.

Alternative:
- muted/dashed.

Rejected:
- caution tone.

## 6. Contextual Evidence Chip

Only appears when needed.

Examples:
- `Wave 1.2 m`
- `SST 27.6°C`
- `Boundary Clear`

Small, not card-like.

## 7. Result Glass Sheet

Appears after query.

Contains:
- recommendation;
- distance;
- safety;
- opportunity;
- confidence;
- 2–4 reasons.

## 8. Section Glass Panel

For inner pages only.

Use sparingly.

A page should not become a card grid.

## 9. Status Dot

Examples:
- LIVE
- CACHED
- DEMO

Keep tiny and semantic.

## 10. Buttons

Primary:
- glass
- soft fill
- no glow.

Secondary:
- transparent
- thin border.
